## Featured Projects

### 1. AI Legal Advisor: Hybrid RAG over 37+ Indian Acts
> Benchmarked, not just built.

- Combines **semantic search, BM25, query expansion, and reranking** to answer legal questions from 37+ Indian Acts.
- Ships with its own **evaluation harness** (Hit@1, Hit@3, Hit@5, MRR) to catch retrieval failures before they turn into wrong answers.

`Python` `ChromaDB` `FastAPI`  ·  [**Repository →**](https://github.com/Janavee01/Legal_Advisor)

---

### 2. FloorPlan AI: 2D Plan to Furnished 3D Render
> From a flat architectural drawing to a photorealistic scene.

- **Multi-pass OCR** extracts room labels across fonts and contrast levels.
- A custom **Voronoi flood-fill** segments open-plan layouts that have no walls between rooms.
- A **ControlNet + Stable Diffusion** stage renders the final furnished scene.

`Python` `OpenCV` `PyTorch` `Stable Diffusion`  ·  [**Repository →**](https://github.com/Janavee01/floorplan)

---

### 3. Real-Time Multiplayer Fighting Game
> Server-authoritative · sub-60ms sync · 95% fewer desyncs

- Async, event-driven networking with **custom state-reconciliation logic** that keeps every client in sync.
- **Load-balanced across game instances with NGINX.**

`Node.js` `Socket.IO` `NGINX`
